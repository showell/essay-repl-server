# Twenty game tie-in themes

*Each theme is a way the book could mention, briefly, that the screensaver could become a game. The pro is why it might work, and the con is why it might not. MISSION C12 now says: with restraint, where the chapter leads to it, and after the book has earned it.*

| Theme | Why it works | Why it doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| **Grab the handlebars** | Engages the reader's imagination, and the throttle is real code. | Treats the reader as a user, not a programmer, and distracts from the mission. |
| **A replay for free** | Determinism is a real strength of Roc, and every game wants replays. | It pitches a feature the ride lacks, and replays only matter once there's input. |
| **One more input** | Each step is already handed a keyboard snapshot, so a game is "one more thing handed in". It teaches the loop. | It's abstract, and it may read as hand-waving about how easy games are. |
| **The bug you can play back** | It speaks to working programmers, who have all chased a once-in-a-thousand bug. | It's a testing story dressed as a game, and it overlaps "A replay for free". |
| **The near miss** | The cat's crossing is already timed to the rider, so the drama exists today. | It's vivid but small, and it risks implying the cat can hit the rider. |
| **Wipe-out into the cows** | It's funny, memorable and charming. | Crashes aren't in the code, and comedy can undercut a technical chapter. |
| **Spot the rare animal** | It ties to the elephants being the fixture, and families get it. | It gives the player no agency: it's watching with a scorecard. |
| **Types as rules of play** | A game's rules are its states, and tag unions make wrong states unwritable. | It needs care to avoid D2, and "rules" means two things here. |
| **Pause and step as a debugger** | The ride already pauses and steps, and it's honest about today. | It's a tooling story, not a game, and stepping is for watching. |
| **Two riders in lockstep** | Determinism makes lockstep netplay real, and it's impressive. | It jumps past simpler one-player ideas and oversells. |
| **Speedrun the safari** | A fixed route and determinism suit speedrunning, and verifying a run is easy. | There's no race and no clock to beat, and it's a niche audience. |
| **Tune the feel** | Throttle and braking limits are plain constants, and tuning them is game design. | It's about config, not Roc, and it's dry without a picture. |
| **The level is data** | The route is a list of segment plans, so a level editor is a list editor. | It's a strong idea for the World chapter but far from the opening, and editors are tools. |
| **Gawking as a mechanic** | The rider already looks at pigs and slows, so distraction is half a game mechanic. | It needs the gaze chapter first, and it could read as a joke. |
| **Day and night as difficulty** | Dusk dims the road, and the headlights matter, so the night is harder. | It's speculative, and it depends on today's dusk change landing. |
| **A screensaver with good timing** | It's honest, warm, and celebrates what the ride is. | It's barely a game tie-in, so it may feel like it dodges the question. |
| **From watching to steering** | It's one clean arc, and it matches the book's own arc from still frames to motion. | It's another flavor of the handlebars, and it shares that theme's distraction. |
| **The platform does the I/O** | A game needs input and sound, and the platform already hands both in, so the pure core stays pure. | It's a platform-chapter idea, and too technical for a teaser. |
| **Every frame is a function** | A game frame is a function of state, which is the book's central theme. | "Frame is a function" is close to a slogan, and it risks an aphorism (D7). |
| **Ship it as a game jam** | It's playful, and it invites the reader to fork the code and try. | It's an outward call to action, and the book's job is reading, not building. |

## Where they might fit

- The **opening** has no room for any of them yet.
- The **pieces as types**, near "Motion": *One more input*, or *Every frame is a function*.
- **Billboard Critters** or the herds: *Spot the rare animal*, at most a sentence.
- **The cat's chapters:** *The near miss*.
- **The rider's chapters:** *Grab the handlebars*, *Tune the feel*, *Gawking as a mechanic*.
- **The ride instant and stepping:** *Pause and step as a debugger*, *A replay for free*.
- **Proving it:** *The bug you can play back*.
- **The platform:** *The platform does the I/O*.

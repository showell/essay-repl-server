# Open calls, round 3

From a fresh boundary review across all of Safari, run after two days of cleanup. None is urgent. A word each is enough. My lean is after each.

1. **Split Geom?** Geom (619 lines, imported by 36 modules) holds two coordinate frames. One is the road frame: `RoadPoint`, `Placed`, `Vantage`, `Bend`, road edges. The other is the rider's view frame: `RiderPoint`, `RaisedPoint`, `AheadPoint`, `LevelPoint`, the near plane, `Region`, clipping. Its own doc names both. Splitting off the view frame (e.g. `ViewPoint`) gives a smaller hub and a clearer doc, but touches about 36 importers, widgets included. *My lean:* yes, split, folded into the big rename lane, since that lane renames the point types anyway.
2. **Where clipping a polygon at the near plane lives.** `Camera.FlatPolygon` and `Camera.clipped_polygon` make Camera, the projection module, import the drawing layer (Polygon, Shapes, RgbColor). They can't go down into Polygon. If you say yes to #1, they join the new view-frame module; otherwise they get a small module of their own. *My lean:* follow #1.
3. **Far projection, x and y, in one place?** Camera has `x_for_turn`/`turn_at_x` (through tan), and Lens has `y_for_height`/`size_on_screen` (a linear scale). Both project things infinitely far, and the Sun uses both. Putting them together in Lens, with FarHeight, would give the cheat one home. Against: they use different models (tan for x, linear for y), which may be intended. *My lean:* together in Lens, with a doc saying why the two axes differ.
4. **Rider (22 lines, one function, one caller).** `Rider.controls`, "the rider's mind", is called only by RiderState. The style rule says a module whose every caller is one other module belongs with that caller. But you liked naming the mind separately, and the rename question (`RiderState` → `Rider`, `Rider` → `RiderMind`) is still open. *My lean:* keep the mind as its own module, and do the rename: the thing is `Rider`, the mind is `RiderMind`.
